Discretion & Protocols

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Detailed assurances concerning safety, bio-security, transparency, and clinical standards.

How does the dedicated Pick & Drop service operate?

expand_more
Furrever Inn provides temperature-controlled pet transport vehicles equipped with secure travel crates, soft bedding, and an accompanied handler. Doorstep pick-up and drop-off can be easily scheduled across Haryana with real-time GPS tracking shared with parents.

How do parents access the 24x7 CCTV live stream and video recordings?

expand_more
Immediately upon check-in, pet parents receive secure encrypted login credentials to our high-definition camera network. You can view crystal-clear live video with night-vision from your phone anytime, plus access recorded playback anytime during the stay.

How often will I receive photo and video updates?

expand_more
Our handlers send regular photo and video journals directly via WhatsApp multiple times daily — capturing meal times, play sessions, grooming, and cozy sleep intervals so you never miss a moment.

What is Furrever Inn's 100% vaccinated companions policy?

expand_more
To protect all resident pets, we enforce an absolute zero-exception vaccination rule. Every pet must have up-to-date vaccination records (DHPPiL, Rabies, and Kennel Cough) checked and verified by our clinical team before entering the premises.

What are the daily sanitization and emergency medical protocols?

expand_more
Our facility undergoes 3x daily hospital-grade sanitization using pet-safe, non-toxic disinfectants. Additionally, we have certified veterinary staff on-site and 24x7 emergency medical transport ready for any immediate health requirement.
